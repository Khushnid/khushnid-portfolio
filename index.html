Now

My focus moved to AI agents.

A chatbot answers. An agent acts: it breaks a goal into steps, calls real tools, reads the results and decides what to do next. I'm exploring how to build that loop so it is reliable enough to trust.

01

Plan

Turn a vague goal into concrete steps, and re-plan when reality disagrees.

02

Act

Call tools and APIs with structured inputs: search, files, code, services.

03

Remember

Keep the right context across steps instead of the whole world in the prompt.

04

Verify

Evals, guardrails and checks, so a result is verified rather than merely confident.
